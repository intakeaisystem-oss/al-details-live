    What a drive-through wash leaves behind.

    A tunnel wash rinses the surface in a few minutes. The things that make a car look tired are the ones it can't reach.

    Embedded dirt and grime

    Brake dust, tar and fallout bond to paint and wheels. They need decontamination and a clay bar, not a rinse.

    Carpets and upholstery

    Spills soak below the surface. Steam and shampoo extraction pull them back out.

    Pet hair and interior buildup

    Hair weaves into carpet fibers, and dust settles in every vent and seam.

    Swirls in the paint

    Spinning brushes put fine scratches in clear coat. Machine polishing takes them out.

    Faded paint, lost gloss

    Oxidation dulls color over time. Correction brings the depth back.

    No lasting protection

    Spray wax is gone in days. A sealant or ceramic coating keeps shedding water and road salt.

    Questions people ask before booking.

    Do you come to my home?

    Yes. A.L Details is fully mobile with no walk-in shop. The detail is done at the address you give when you book: your driveway, home or office. The vehicle needs to be accessible, with room to open all the doors.

    Do you need my water or power?

    An outlet and a water source help when they're available, and a garage or carport is a plus. If there's no water on site, Aiden can bring his own.

    How long does detailing take?

    It depends on the vehicle's size, its condition and the service. A full detail on a family SUV can run close to three hours. You get an arrival window when your booking is confirmed.

    What is the difference between a detail and a car wash?

    A wash cleans the surface. A detail goes after what's bonded to the paint and soaked into the interior: decontamination and clay bar outside, vacuuming, steam and shampoo extraction inside, then protection on top so it lasts.

    What does paint correction do?

    It machine-polishes the clear coat to remove swirl marks and light scratches and bring back gloss. Detailing is restorative, not repair: deep paint defects, rock chips and rust can't be polished out.

    How long does ceramic coating last?

    Aiden offers two ceramic coating packages, rated for up to 3 years and up to 5 years. How long it actually lasts depends on how the vehicle is washed and stored.

    Do you detail SUVs and trucks?

    Yes. Cars, trucks, SUVs and vans. Oversized or heavily modified vehicles may be quoted separately.

    Book online, or call or text (224) 291-2987. Your price is based on vehicle size, the service and condition. Things like pet hair or heavy soiling can add to it, and any extra charge is quoted and approved before work begins.
